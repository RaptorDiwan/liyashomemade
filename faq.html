👶 Baby Food & Toddler Guidance
Our Baby Instant Health Mix and Baby Rice Cereal are formulated to be gentle enough for babies starting from 6 months of age (once your baby has begun solid foods). The ingredients (badam, cashew, fried gram dal, makhana, white poha, red poha) are carefully roasted and stone-ground to an ultra-fine powder so tiny tummies digest them smoothly.
Take 1 to 2 tablespoons of Baby Instant Health Mix in a small pan. Add 1 cup of cold water or diluted milk and stir well without lumps. Place on low flame and cook for 3 to 5 minutes, stirring continuously until it turns into a smooth, silky porridge. You can sweeten it lightly with pure jaggery syrup or mashed banana. Allow it to cool to lukewarm before feeding.

🏷️ Shelf Life & Proper Storage
Because our mixes are 100% natural and contain zero preservatives or chemical extenders, the recommended shelf life is 3 to 4 months at room temperature in an airtight container, and up to 6 months if refrigerated. Always use a dry spoon.
Our pouches come with an airtight resealable zip-lock. After taking the required powder, press out excess air and seal the zip firmly. Alternatively, transfer the powder into a clean, dry glass or stainless steel container kept away from moisture and direct sunlight.
