Return, Refund & Cancellation Policy

At Liya's Homemade, we take utmost care in packing and shipping every order. As our products are food and grocery items, returns are not available once the order has been delivered. However, if there is a genuine issue with your order, we are here to help.

Cases Eligible for Refund

You may be eligible for a refund only in the following situations:

  • Product received is damaged, leaking, torn, or tampered
  • Product received is expired
  • Wrong product was delivered
  • Item is missing from the delivered package
  • Product has a genuine quality issue, subject to verification by our team

Reporting Time & Required Proof

⚠️ Reporting Window: Any issue must be reported to Liya's Homemade within 12 hours of delivery.

To help us investigate quickly, customers must share the following details:

  • Order number
  • Product name
  • Clear photos of the product and outer package
  • Mandatory unboxing video
  • Short description of the issue
Mandatory Unboxing Video: An unboxing video is mandatory for the refund process. Refund requests will be considered only if a clear unboxing video is shared at the time of raising the complaint. Requests made without an unboxing video will not be eligible for refund.

Verification Process

Once we receive your complaint, our team will review the details and supporting proof.

  • Verification is generally completed within 24 business hours.
  • After verification, Liya's Homemade may offer a Full refund or Partial refund.
  • The decision will be based on the issue reported and the evidence shared.

Return Not Available

As Liya's Homemade deals in food and grocery products, returns are not available once the order has been delivered.

Non-Refundable Cases

Refunds will not be accepted in the following cases:

  • Change of mind after delivery
  • Taste preference or personal liking/disliking
  • Incorrect address provided by the customer
  • Failed delivery due to customer unavailability
  • Damage caused after delivery
  • Complaint raised without adequate proof
  • Complaint raised without the mandatory unboxing video
  • Complaint raised after the allowed reporting period

Order Cancellation

Order cancellation is not available once the transaction is completed.

Refund Method

  • Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method used while placing the order.
  • Refunds are usually completed within 5 to 7 business days after approval, depending on the payment provider or bank.

Incorrect Address Policy

Customers are requested to enter the correct shipping address and contact details at the time of placing the order. Liya's Homemade will not be responsible for delivery issues, delays, or losses arising due to incorrect or incomplete address details provided by the customer.
